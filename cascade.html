Pixxel Space — Media Kit 2025

Seeing the unseen.
From orbit.

Pixxel is a space data company and spacecraft manufacturer building a health monitor for the planet. Our constellation of hyperspectral satellites captures data across 150+ spectral bands — revealing what conventional satellites can't see. The Aurora platform turns this data into actionable planetary intelligence for agriculture, forestry, energy, defence, and climate.
