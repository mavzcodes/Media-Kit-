What We Do

Pixxel is building a health monitor for the Earth. We design, build, and operate hyperspectral imaging satellites that can detect phenomena invisible to conventional cameras — from crop stress and water quality to mineral deposits and carbon sequestration.

Our constellation of satellites captures the planet in over 250 spectral bands, delivering actionable intelligence to industries including agriculture, environmental monitoring, mining, and defence.

The Mission

We believe access to accurate, high-frequency Earth observation data can fundamentally change how humanity manages its resources. Every dataset we produce is a step toward a world that can see its problems clearly enough to solve them.

Key Numbers

Founded in 2019 in Bangalore, India. 200+ employees across India and the US. $95M+ raised. 3 satellites in orbit. 250+ spectral bands. Customers in 15 countries.

Founded 2019 Series B Bangalore · San Francisco ISRO Partner

Founders

Awais Ahmed (CEO) and Kshitij Khandelwal (CTO) met at BITS Pilani while competing in satellite design competitions. They founded Pixxel with the conviction that hyperspectral imaging should be accessible, not a privilege of government programmes.
